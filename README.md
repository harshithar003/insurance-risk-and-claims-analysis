# 🚗 Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis

This project analyzes car insurance policyholders, their vehicles, and claim history using **Power BI**.  
It provides insights into customer demographics, vehicle details, and claim behavior to help insurers make data-driven decisions.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview
The dataset includes:
- **Customer Demographics**: Birthdate, Gender, Marital Status, Education, Household Income, Parental Status
- **Vehicle Details**: Car Make, Model, Color, Year of Manufacture, Usage Type (Personal, Commercial, Commute)
- **Claims Data**: Claim Amount, Claim Frequency, Kids Driving (number of young drivers in household)
- **Coverage Zone**: Geographic risk area (Urban, Rural, Zone A, etc.)

---

## 🎯 Business Requirements
The dashboard was designed to meet the following KPIs:
- **Total Policies** – Active customer base size  
- **Total Claim Amount** – Overall financial impact of claims  
- **Claim Frequency** – How often claims are filed  
- **Average Claim Amount** – Severity and risk exposure  
- **Gender-wise Policies** – Customer distribution across genders  

---

## 📈 Visualizations
The Power BI report includes:
- **Car Use (Donut Chart)** – Policy distribution & claims by usage type  
- **Car Make (Bar Chart)** – Claims by car brand  
- **Coverage Zone (Donut Chart)** – Regional risk analysis  
- **Age Group (Histogram)** – Claims by age bracket  
- **Car Year (Area Chart)** – Impact of vehicle age on claims  
- **Kids Driving (Ribbon Chart)** – Household risk with young drivers  
- **Education (Pie Chart)** – Education level vs policy adoption  
- **Education & Marital Status (Heat Grid)** – Combined demographic analysis
